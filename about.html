About this collection

The festival

EXPRMNTL was an international festival for experimental film, established by Jacques Ledoux and the Royal Belgian Film Archive, co-organized with Pierre Vermeylen. Held in a vacant casino in Knokke-le-Zoute between Christmas and New Year, it ran for only five editions — 1949, 1958, 1963, 1967 and 1974 — yet became one of the few meeting points for avant-garde filmmakers from across the world, and is widely regarded as the most important event ever organized for experimental cinema.

This project

This site gathers photographs, programmes and digitized documents related to the festival into a single, browsable collection. It is a work in progress: most sections currently hold placeholder structure rather than final material, and are being filled in as items are digitized, catalogued and verified.
