Portraits for directors go here, named after the director's slug (the part
of their URL after people/), followed by a two-digit number:

  norman-mclaren-01.jpg → first portrait for people/norman-mclaren.html
  norman-mclaren-02.jpg → second portrait for the same person
  agnes-varda-01.jpg    → for people/agnes-varda.html

To find a director's slug, open their page and look at the address bar, or
check people.html — the filename before ".html" is the slug.

WHAT HAPPENS AUTOMATICALLY, based on how many numbered files you add:

- Add just "-01.jpg": it becomes the portrait shown on the director's page.
- Add "-02.jpg" and onward too: the "Portrait photographs" row in that
  director's Resources list turns into a "View Gallery" button on its own,
  opening a lightbox with all the numbered photos you've added, in order.

No HTML editing needed either way, and you only ever upload each file once.
Always start at -01 with no gaps (01, 02, 03…) — the site stops looking as
soon as a number is missing.

Recommended format: .jpg, roughly 1200px on the longest side is plenty for
the web (no need to upload full-resolution scans here — keep those in your
own archive).
