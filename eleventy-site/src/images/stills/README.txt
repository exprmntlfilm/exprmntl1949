Stills for films go here, named after the film's slug (the part of its URL
after films/), followed by a two-digit number:

  hen-hop-01.jpg          → first still for films/cat-001.html (Hen Hop)
  hen-hop-02.jpg          → second still for the same film
  la-pointe-courte-01.jpg → for films/la-pointe-courte.html

To find a film's slug, open its page and look at the address bar, or check
films.html — the filename before ".html" is the slug.

WHAT HAPPENS AUTOMATICALLY, based on how many numbered files you add:

- Add just "-01.jpg": it becomes the cover image, shown on the film's own
  page, on its card in films.html, and on every director's page that lists
  this film.
- Add "-02.jpg" and onward too: the "Film stills" row in the film's
  Resources list turns into a "View Gallery" button on its own, opening a
  lightbox with all the numbered photos you've added, in order.

No HTML editing needed either way, and you only ever upload each file once.
Always start at -01 with no gaps (01, 02, 03…) — the site stops looking as
soon as a number is missing.

Recommended format: .jpg, roughly 1200px on the longest side is plenty for
the web (no need to upload full-resolution scans here — keep those in your
own archive).
