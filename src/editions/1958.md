year: 1958
label: EXPRMNTL 2
blurb: "After a hiatus of almost a decade, Jacques Ledoux organised the second
  edition of EXPRMNTL in 1958, as part of the famous Brussels World Fair. For
  this reason, this was the only edition in which the festival did not take
  place in Knokke, but in the Belgian capital instead. On this occasion, the
  retrospective format gave way to a film competition in its own right, with
  many of the entries having been directed by as yet unknown filmmakers. The
  second edition of EXPRMNTL also opened the doors to a greater diversity of
  countries: nations such as Brazil, Japan, Argentina, Mexico and Poland had
  directors taking part in the competition for the first time, some of whom
  received official awards. The programme ran from 21 to 25 April 1958. On the
  final day, the jury – comprising Alexandre Alexeieff, John Grierson, Norman
  McLaren, Curt Oertel, Pierre Prévert, Man Ray and Edgar Varèse – awarded the
  official prizes to Walerian Borowczyk, Jan Lenica, Len Lye, Yoram Gross, Roman
  Polanski, Hy Hirsch, Hilary Harris, Francis Thompson and Rodolfo Kuhn."
