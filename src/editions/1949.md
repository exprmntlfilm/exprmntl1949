label: EXPRMNTL 1
blurb: "Organised as part of the Deuxième Festival du Film et des Beaux-Arts de
  Belgique, the first edition of EXPRMNTL took place between 25 June and 8 July
  1949. Jacques Ledoux, curator at the Cinematek Royale de Belgique, wished to
  present a historical retrospective of avant-garde cinema, whilst also
  providing an opportunity for new directors and artists. Consequently, this
  inaugural edition was unique in that it featured films both in and out of
  competition: established figures such as Germaine Dulac, Luis Buñuel and Jean
  Cocteau had works screened throughout the programme, but were not in the
  running for any awards. Only films produced from 1940 onwards were eligible to
  compete, which meant that names such as Norman McLaren and Oskar Fischinger
  were able to make their mark."
