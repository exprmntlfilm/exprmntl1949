blurb: Organised as part of the Deuxième Festival du Film et des Beaux-Arts de
  Belgique, the first edition of EXPRMNTL took place between 25 June and 8 July
  1949. Jacques Ledoux, curator at the Cinematek Royale de Belgique, wished to
  present a historical retrospective of avant-garde cinema, whilst also
  providing an opportunity for new directors and artists. Consequently, this
  inaugural edition featured films out of competition by established figures
  such as Germaine Dulac, Luis Buñuel and Jean Cocteau. Only films produced from
  1940 onwards were eligible to compete, which meant that names such as Norman
  McLaren and Oskar Fischinger were able to make their mark.
