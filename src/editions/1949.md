blurb: Organised as part of the Deuxième Festival du Film et des Beaux-Arts de
  Belgique, the first edition of EXPRMNTL took place between 25 June and 8 July
  1949 at the Knokke casino. Jacques Ledoux, curator at the Cinémathèque royale
  de Belgique, wished to present a historical retrospective of avant-garde
  cinema, whilst also providing an opportunity for new directors and artists to
  showcase their works. Consequently, this inaugural edition featured films out
  of competition by established figures such as Germaine Dulac, Luis Buñuel,
  Walter Ruttmann, etc. Only films produced from 1940 onwards were eligible to
  compete, which meant that names such as Norman McLaren and Oskar Fischinger
  were able to make their mark. At the end of the competition, the official
  prizes were awarded to Oskar Fischinger, Eli Lotar, the brothers James and
  John Whitney, Luigi Veronesi and Norman McLaren.
