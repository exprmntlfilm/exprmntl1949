label: EXPRMNTL 3
blurb: >-
  In 1963, the now well-established EXPRMNTL returned to its original venue, the
  Knokke casino. This edition also established a new format that would be used
  until the very last festival: the event would always take place between
  Christmas and New Year. Having brought together a new crop of avant-garde film
  talent, EXPRMNTL 3 featured a strong contingent of American filmmakers,
  including Kenneth Anger, Gregory Markopoulos, Madeline Tourtelot and even the
  future director of commercial hits, Brian De Palma. Invited to serve on the
  jury, Jonas Mekas was also in attendance, alongside his friend P. Adams
  Sitney. The event ended up being marred by accusations of censorship levelled
  at the organisers, who banned the screening of Jack Smith’s film "Flaming
  Creatures". This caused a great stir amongst the participants, who decided to
  organise private screenings of the film as a form of protest. Jonas Mekas, in
  particular, was deeply troubled by the situation and resigned from the
  position he had been offered. 

  At the end of the festival, a jury comprising James Broughton, Jean Cayrol, William Klein, Jan Lenica, Lorenza Mazzetti, Norman McLaren, Jørgen Roos and Herbert Vesely awarded prizes to the following directors: Ferdinand Kittl, Stan VanDerBeek, Gregory Markopoulos, Alexandre Alexeieff, Claire Parker, Walerian Borowczyk, Claude Jutra and Vlado Kristl. In recognition of his work, the committee awarded Jack Smith the special "film maudit" prize for "Flaming Creatures".
