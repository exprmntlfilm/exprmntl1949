blurb: >-
  The latest edition of EXPRMNTL took place between 25 December 1974 and 2
  January 1975, and once again broadened the festival’s scope to include other
  forms of artistic expression, such as political music, theatre and the
  emerging art form of video art. With fewer films in competition than in
  previous editions, it once again featured a strong contingent of American
  directors. Belgium, England and Germany were also well represented.

  Although less controversial than the previous edition, EXPRMNTL 4 featured a screening of Thierry Zeno’s film *Vase de Noces*, which shocked audiences by depicting a romantic relationship between a man and a pig.

  At the end of the competition, Dore O., Tsuneo Nakai, Michio Okabe, Anna Ambrose, Anthony McCall, Coleen Fitzgibbon, Mike Dunford, Thierry Zeno, J. J. Murphy, Richard Woolley, Jean Van Helden and Claudine Eizykman received awards for their work.

  More than two decades later, EXPRMNTL saw its final edition come to an end. By then, the international avant-garde film scene was completely different from what it had been in 1949. Now, with internationally recognised directors and closer ties to other art forms, it came to be recognised as an artistic medium in its own right. Although many wonder why EXPRMNTL did not take place more often, perhaps the very fact that it was a sporadic festival is precisely why it was such an exclusive, artistically rich event, imbued with a unique spirit of cooperation amongst its participants.
