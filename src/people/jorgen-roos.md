Jørgen Roos (1922–1998) was a Danish experimental filmmaker, photographer and documentary filmmaker, born in Gilleleje. His brother was Karl Roos, who wrote the first Danish book on film theory, *Film* (1936).

Although he made a number of institutional films, mainly for educational and military purposes, Jørgen Roos also devoted himself to avant-garde cinema, primarily in collaboration with his friend Albert Mertz. Together, they filmed *Flugten* in 1942, which marked his debut in experimental cinema. In 1948, he worked as director of photography and editor on the film *They Reached the Ferry*, by the acclaimed director Carl Theodor Dreyer. His relationship with Dreyer proved fruitful, and after a few further collaborations, he directed a documentary about his colleague in 1966.

In 1949, Jørgen Roos and Albert Mertz took part in the first edition of EXPRMNTL, having directed two films together.
