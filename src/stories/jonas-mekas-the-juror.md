In 1963, Jacques Ledoux invited the celebrated avant-garde film director Jonas Mekas to be one of the jurors at the third edition of EXPRMNTL. Jonas was keen, as he knew the festival was one of the few that genuinely took an interest in experimental films and enabled young filmmakers to launch their careers.
