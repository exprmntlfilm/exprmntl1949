Explore the stories, photographs and film collections of EXPRMNTL, Knokke-le-Zoute.

EXPRMNTL was an international festival for experimental film, founded by Jacques Ledoux and the Royal Belgian Film Archive. Held between Christmas and New Year in a vacant casino on the Belgian coast, it became one of the few places where the world's avant-garde filmmakers actually met — and, for a few years, the closest thing experimental cinema had to a capital.

The collection

Photographs, programmes, and documents — gathered in one place

EXPRMNTL is regarded as an important breeding ground for experimental film: one of the few venues where avant-garde movements from across Europe and North America actually crossed paths. Beyond screenings, the festival hosted happenings, performances and — famously — its share of controversy.

This site brings together digitized programmes, photographs, press clippings and other traces of the five editions. Most items below are placeholders: the structure is ready, and material is being added as it is digitized and catalogued.
